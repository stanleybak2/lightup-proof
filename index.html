Proof

How this works
  • The game has three rules. A numbered wall has exactly that many bulbs next to it. Every white cell must be lit. No bulb may shine on another bulb.
  • You never pick a rule from a menu. Click the clue that is your reason: a number, a bulb, or a white cell (meaning "this cell must be lit"). The cells that rule talks about get a dashed outline.
  • Then mark a cell. Left-click uses the "Mark as" setting; right-click always marks empty. The step is accepted only if that one rule forces it. Esc drops the cited clue.
  • Suppose opens an assumption. Inside it everything is hypothetical (purple). If a rule becomes impossible, click that clue and press "This rule is broken": the assumption closes and its opposite is proved.
  • Split cell or "Cases from this rule" opens side-by-side cases. Click a case to work in it. A case can die by contradiction. "End cases" keeps whatever is true in every surviving case.
