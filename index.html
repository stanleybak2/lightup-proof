Game rules
  • Place light bulbs in white cells.
  • A bulb lights its own cell and every cell in its row and column, up to the nearest wall.
  • Every white cell must be lit.
  • No bulb may shine on another bulb.
  • A numbered wall must have exactly that many bulbs in the cells directly above, below, left and right of it.
  • A wall without a number can have any number of bulbs next to it.
Proof controls
  • Every mark needs a reason. Click the clue that is your reason. It can be a number, or a white cell, which means "this cell must be lit". The cells that rule talks about get a dashed outline.
  • Then mark an outlined cell. "Mark as" shows which mouse button places a bulb and which marks a cell empty. Select the other one to swap them. The step is accepted only if that one rule forces it. Esc drops the cited clue.
  • Yellow cells are lit. A lit cell can never hold a bulb, so it counts as empty without a step.
  • Undo takes back one action at a time.
  • Assume opens an assumption, shown in purple. If a rule becomes impossible inside it, click that clue and press "This rule is broken". The assumption closes and its opposite is proved.
  • Case split and "Cases from this rule" open cases side by side. Click a case to work in it. A contradiction closes a case. "End cases" keeps whatever is true in every open case.
